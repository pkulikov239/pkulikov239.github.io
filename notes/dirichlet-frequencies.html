Dirichlet frequencies on an interval

How the length and a constant wave speed enter the resonance frequencies of a string.

Normal modes

Let L>0L>0 and let c0>0c_0>0 be constant. Seek solutions of the form u(t,x)=cos⁡(ωt)ϕ(x)u(t,x)=\cos(\omega t)\phi(x) on (0,L)(0,L) with zero Dirichlet boundary values. The spatial factor satisfies

−ϕ′′(x)=ω2c02ϕ(x),ϕ(0)=ϕ(L)=0.-\phi''(x)=\frac{\omega^2}{c_0^2}\phi(x),\qquad \phi(0)=\phi(L)=0.

For each positive integer nn, the corresponding mode and positive angular frequency are

ϕn(x)=sin⁡(nπxL),ωn=nπc0L.\phi_n(x)=\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right),\qquad \omega_n=\frac{n\pi c_0}{L}.

What can be recovered?

If LL is known, the first frequency already determines the constant speed.

c0=Lω1π.c_0=\frac{L\omega_1}{\pi}.

If both c0c_0 and LL are unknown, simultaneous scaling (c0,L)↦(ac0,aL)(c_0,L)\mapsto(ac_0,aL) for a>0a>0 preserves every frequency. The sequence determines the travel time L/c0L/c_0.
